About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Biological Systems Engineering at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Zhou Zhang, with a minor in Computer Sciences. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research focuses on geospatial AI, remote sensing, and knowledge-guided machine learning for agricultural and environmental systems. I am particularly interested in connecting scientific knowledge and multimodal data with deployable machine learning systems, from crop-yield modeling to interactive geospatial tools.
